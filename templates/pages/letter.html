✧   FOR MY ONE AND ONLY   ✧

My beautiful Bangaram,

Happy 20th Birthday, my love! Today, more than anything, I wish I could be beside you. I wish I could hold your hand, look into your eyes, see your smile, and tell you how much I love you.

Even though I can't be with you today, I want you to know something: you are the only girl I want. You are the only girl I love. And you are the only girl I want to keep choosing, every single day.

Before you came into my life, I never imagined one person could become so important to me. But then you happened, Bangaram. Your smile became my favorite sight, your voice became my favorite sound, and talking to you became one of the most beautiful parts of my day.

It's only been four months since our story began, but those four months have meant so much to me. My heart doesn't measure love in months or years. It remembers the little moments, the conversations, the smiles, and the happiness you've brought into my life.

I don't want a thousand love stories. I just want one. One love. One girl. One beautiful story. And I want that girl to be you.

I want to celebrate your happiest moments and stand beside you when life feels difficult. I want to support your dreams, listen to your worries, and remind you how special you are whenever you forget. I'm not perfect, but I promise to keep learning, understanding you, respecting you, and doing my best to love you the way you deserve.

Just because I'm not beside you doesn't mean you're far from my heart. If I could, I'd cross every mile between us just to see you smile today.

Since I can't be there, I made this little magical garden for you. Every flower, every butterfly, every glowing star, every photograph, and every tiny surprise — I made all of it thinking about you.

I hope life gives you countless reasons to smile. I hope your dreams find their way into reality, and I hope you always feel beautiful, confident, appreciated, and loved.

Among all the people in this world, my heart chose you. And if I had to choose all over again, I'd still choose you.

One day, Bangaram, I hope I won't have to wish you happy birthday through a screen. I hope I'll be standing right beside you, holding your hand and watching you smile.

Until then, please remember: you are my favorite person, my beautiful Bangaram, my one and only love.

Happy 20th Birthday, Sreya Chandana. Thank you for making these four months so beautiful. I love you more than these words can explain.

Forever yours,
Your boy ♡
P.S. If you could see yourself through my eyes, you'd understand how incredibly special you are to me. 💙
